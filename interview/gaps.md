# Gaps Queue

The normalized queue of unresolved facts, people, relationships, scenes, and coverage debts. `coverage.md` explains why a gap matters; this file tracks what remains open. `state.md` is the only live-status source.

Priority: **P0** (the record is structurally incomplete without it) · **P1** (a major world is thin) · **P2** (texture). Type: `person | relationship | scene | era | world | fact | form | process | safety | meta`. Status is free prose but should begin with `open`, `partial`, or a closing word.
