## Minors
Minor children are never exported without the consent of a parent or guardian, and never in a form that identifies them beyond what that consent covers.

## Third Parties in Unflattering Episodes
Where the record renders a living person in a conflict, a failure, or a private matter, that content is the subject's framing only. On any export, default to role labels ("a step-sibling," "a business partner") rather than names, unless that person has consented.

## Health, Therapy, Legal, Financial
Private categories. Renderable in the record at the subject's discretion; specifics require explicit per-item consent before any export.

## Publication Gate
Nothing leaves this repo — no export, share, reading copy, or publication — until every named living person in the exported text has a decision row above, and every off-limits item has been checked against the text.
