# Hypotheses Ledger

Live claims under test. Maintained during recalibration and by the Synthesis Pass.

## Rules
- A hypothesis may only **inform** a question that still passes **Earned**. "It tests H-n" is never sufficient.
- **Cap:** no more than 1 of the last 3 questions may be primarily hypothesis-aimed.
- Cite evidence with source Qs. Status: `open | supported | weakened | retired`.
- Never write a hypothesis into `cast.md` or `facts.md` on the same line as a fact.

## Entry format

```
## H-<id>: <claim>
Evidence for: [F-Qn, I-Qn, ...]
What they might say that would change this reading: <…>
Status: open | supported | weakened | retired
Question idea (must be Earned): <optional>
```
