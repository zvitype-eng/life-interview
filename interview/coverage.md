# Coverage Governance

Tracks whether the interview has enough of the *life* — not whether the questions were good. A beautiful question can still miss the life.

## Phase Closure Gates

### Phase 1 Gate: Life Architecture Skeleton
Before leaving Architecture, the interviewer can answer: Who are the primary living people in the subject's life now? What households did the subject belong to? What is daily life now — partnership, children, work, community, money, leisure, body? What are the major arcs — geographic, educational, career, relational, religious or cultural? Which cast clusters remain unnamed?

### Phase 2 Gate: Structural Worlds Rendered
Each major world has at least one scene, one relationship dynamic, one current-state note, and one open question. Worlds to check: family of origin; extended or blended family; partnership; children; siblings; religious or cultural world; schools and institutions; work; present-day household; private desire; money and class; friendship and community.

### Phase 3 Gate: Interior Distributed
Interiority sampled across the subject's major identities — partner, parent, child, sibling, cultural adult, worker or maker, friend, private dreamer, current-age self. No single thread consumes the slots the others need.

### Phase 4 Gate: Present-State Completeness
A normal weekday; a normal rest day; partnership now; parenting or care now; who calls them; whom they call; what they want next; what they fear losing; the belief or community frame they live inside. The present is what the record looks back from.

## Hard Cast Gate
Before any phase closes, every major family or household cluster is named, intentionally deferred with a reason, or marked not needed.
