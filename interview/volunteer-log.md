# Volunteer Log — verbatim home for subject-led material

The permanent **verbatim** record for anything the subject volunteers outside a numbered question. Parallel to `log.md`. Append-only. Entries are keyed `## V-<date>-<n>`.

Discipline:
- **Verbatim blocks are quote-only.** Never interleave agent gloss inside a quote.
- Agent framing goes under a separate `[paraphrase / agent note]` line.
- No interpretation here — that lives in the topical files.
- Where the subject volunteered the *existence* of something but their words were not captured, record it as a **re-ask candidate** — never reconstruct fake verbatim.

The doctor requires every `F-vol-<date>` tag used in the topical files to have a `## V-<date>-N` entry here.
