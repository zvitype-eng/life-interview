# Pre-Interview Context (up to 5 questions)

These calibrate the interviewer's role before Q1. They do not count toward the 100.

Suggested calibration questions (ask what fits; keep them plain):
- Who is this record for — you, your children, a wider audience?
- Are there people, periods, or topics that are off-limits? (Record the answer in `privacy.md`.)
- If this is ever written up: how much license — none, narrow, or open? (Default is none.)
- What would you be most disappointed to see missing?

Format per entry:
```
## P<n> — <ISO date>
**Q:** ...
**A:** ...
```
