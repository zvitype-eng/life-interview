# Form Readiness

If this record is ever written up, this file tracks which forms the material could support — and, more importantly, keeps the interview from narrowing early because one scene is strong.

## Rule

Do not decide a form because one scene is strong. Track evidence, missing material, and risks. Update every 10 questions and at session end.
